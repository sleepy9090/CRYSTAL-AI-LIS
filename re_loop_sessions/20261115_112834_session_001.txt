**Which code uses which banks:** I checked all 48 places that call `SwitchBank16` and put the full table in Key Findings. The main points:
- Banks 26/27 are used most, mainly for fades and per-player (X=0/1) routines.
- Banks 18/19 hold main states 2 and 3, the cold-boot init, and a shared routine at `$BA65`.
- Banks 16/17 hold main states 4 and 5.
