I finished the session-77 task, the Ph3 world-interaction leftovers, all parts (a)–(g). Everything was checked in the project's own emulator (tools.emu), not against FCEUX. Most of the documented rules held; two were wrong and a few new quirks turned up.

**Matched the documented model:**
- **Walls:** the player's collision box starts at x−4, as documented.
- **Camera:** the follow dead zones, the 1 px/frame drift back to centre, the view-offset ramp and all four clamps behave as written. The clamps were tested by poking the camera out of range, since the open part of the test area doesn't reach the edges.
- **Enemy contact (b):** HP drops by attack − defence (48 → 27 in the test). Then come 30 frames of invulnerability and a knockback of 7 frames × 5 px. When defence ≥ attack, or the damage is ≥ 128, you get sound `$3A` and no damage, but the knockback still happens.
- **Hazards (d):** a damage floor takes 4 HP every 32 frame-counter ticks, which is 34 real frames because the hit frame and the palette-restore frame don't tick the counter. Area `$1A` hurts every 10 frames. The two accessories (`$13`, `$0D`) block these as documented.
- **Pit fall (c):** in a pit area with an exit record, the player drops, lands in area `$69` and falls in from above as described.

**Corrections:**
- **Low obstacles (`$02` tiles) block a normal jump too.** The normal jump never changes the collision mask, so only the flight magic and the warp-exit status can cross these tiles. REVERSE.md and `docs/bg_coll.txt` now say so.
- **The sinking "empty" sprite (`$4C`) shows on the ground in a sink tile, not in the air.**

**New findings:**
- **Fall-in height table sign slip:** entry `$2F` is +118 where −118 was meant. For one frame the player sprite is drawn 118 px *below* the player, off-screen in my test. The web port reads this table from the ROM, so it keeps the glitch.
- **Endless pit loop:** areas `$91`, `$B9` and `$BA` have pit tiles but no record for where a pit leads. Standing on one makes the game fall, fade back in, and fall again forever. I didn't check whether a player can actually reach those tiles.
- **Currents always push the player down**, because the player object starts with direction 4 and nothing changes it. Holding DOWN jumps the current's speed to 6; walking against it stalls at speed 6 and is pushed back from 7.
- **Player level `$11` (e):** normal levelling stops at `$10`, so `$11` is a developer-only invincibility value that only a RAM or save edit can set.
- **Debug cheat:** the A+B-at-boot flag does have one reader, a cheat at `15:BF47` that fills the inventory and maxes level and MP. It is switched off by a single `RTS` byte at `15:BF46`, so it never runs.
- **Object AIs (e):** AIs `$0A`/`$0B`/`$52` are the sparring duel, not "player held". AI `$38` is a platform that shuttles across a pit, and `$3C` is a one-use platform that disappears after you ride it. The status messages `$19`–`$1B` are poisoned / paralyzed / turned to stone.

**Tools:**
- **New `tools/emu/world_probe.py`:** from a save state it can poke an area, poke RAM (once, every frame, or at a given frame) and play button segments. It then logs the player, camera, HP and state per frame, and can print the live collision map, the cells around the player, and the active objects.
- **New `--coll` option in `tools/render_level.py`** (task part f): it writes a tinted collision PNG plus a per-cell text map for each area. The text uses the same characters as the probe's live map, so the web port's collision map can be compared area by area. For area `$6A`, the rows I compared matched the emulator.
