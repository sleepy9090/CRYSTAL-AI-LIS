**What I found:**
- **The lag rule:** a main-loop pass lags exactly when its cycles, not counting the NMI it overran, are more than (503 − S) × 341/3, where S is the line the pass started on. I tested this on the full 480-frame runs: area `$81` has 461 passes and 18 LAG, area `$59` has 468 passes and 11 LAG. Every pass falls on the correct side, and the gap between the closest LAG and non-LAG pass is under 100 cycles. A LAG pass's total includes the ~1300-cycle NMI it ran into, which is why s210 never saw a pass end on lines 241-250.
- **The start line comes from the previous NMI's length**, in fixed steps: about 1300 cycles → L252, 1610 → L255, 1820 → L257, 2000 → L258. The port's NMI-time count predicts that length to within 14 cycles rms on the area the fit hadn't seen (`$5A`).
