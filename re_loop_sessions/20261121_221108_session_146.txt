**What the port does** (`web/src/game/states/changeform.js`):
- **Menu:** it opens the "What form will you take?" window and shows the player plus 4 form icons. LEFT and RIGHT move the cursor with a sound and wrap around after 4 forms. B, SELECT or START cancel without spending anything.
- **Choosing a form (A):** a 31-frame preview animation of the chosen form, then a 32-frame wait. It then records the form in `obj1_flash` (`$0710`): b7 marks a changed form, b5-4 hold which one. It spends 20 MP (`$14`) and returns to play.
- **Reuse:** it reuses the window, cursor and exit helpers from `telepathy.js`, which I made exportable.
- **Wiring:** it is registered as entry 23 in `play.js`, plus an `ENTRY_POINTS['30:DEC1']` entry so saved states load cleanly.

**Results against tools.emu:**
- **Frame by frame:** all 183 frames match with no offset, covering the window, menu, preview, wait and exit. The telepathy menu needed a 1-frame offset for most of its run; this one doesn't.
- **Skipped columns:** sprite slot `$1F` (tools.emu records it in the middle of the sprite draw), and the palette on two frames (f2555 when the window opens, f2724 at the exit), where tools.emu and the port upload it one frame apart.
- **RAM at f2727:** MP (`$60` → `$4C`), `obj1_flash` and the restored `irq_mode` are equal. Only 8 scratch / PPU-queue bytes differ.
- **Other cases checked:** form 2 applied, LEFT wrapping from form 0 to form 3 and applying it, and B cancel keeping MP and the form bits.
- **After the exit:** play is not compared. The town trigger re-runs the item-give step, and the NPCs are one step apart because of the known lag-frame timing gap.

**Two notes:**
- **Form bits are ORed in, not replaced:** `obj1_flash` is never cleared here, so changing form twice mixes the old and new form bits. The port does the same.
- **Unreachable stack bug:** if MP were short at the final spend, the ROM would corrupt its stack. It can't happen because MP is already checked when magic 7 is cast. The port just logs it.
