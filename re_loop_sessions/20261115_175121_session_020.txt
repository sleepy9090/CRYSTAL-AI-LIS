**Controller input:** I found the pad-reading code, which also finishes the separate "identify controller read routine" task.
- `ReadPadRaw` `31:FF28` reads both pads. The button bits are A=b7, B=b6, Select=b5, Start=b4, then Up, Down, Left, Right. `ReadPadStable` `31:FF17` reads each pad until two reads agree.
- `31:FE80` was labelled `ColdInitSub2`, but it is the per-frame gameplay pad read, now `ReadPads`. It sets:
  - `$43` previous buttons
  - `$4B` newly pressed buttons
  - `$49` an 8-way direction, from the table at `31:FED0`
  - `$4D` new presses plus two hold bits: A held for 31+ frames, and a B pulse every 32 frames
- `ReadPadsMenu` `31:FEE0` is the menu version. A held d-pad repeats after 12 frames, then every 3 frames.

**Session-19 questions:**
- **`$4B` bit 6 is "B newly pressed".** `26:9B45` turned out to be the player's update routine (the AI for object type `$04`), now labelled `PlayerUpdate`. Its first check means pressing B uses the selected item.
