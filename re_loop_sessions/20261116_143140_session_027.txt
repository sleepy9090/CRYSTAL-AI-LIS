I finished **Ph4 assets 5/5**: the asset browser at `web/catalog.html`. I could only test it headless, because there's no browser on this machine. Nobody has looked at it on screen or heard the sound output yet.

**To run it:** `python3 tools/catalog_export.py`, then `python3 -m http.server` in the repo root, then open `http://localhost:8000/web/catalog.html`.

**Tabs**
- **CHR:** all 128 graphics banks, drawn straight from the ROM's graphics data. You can view them in grayscale or any of the 256 palettes, and click a bank to enlarge it.
- **Palettes:** the 256 palettes and the 64-colour master palette. Hovering a palette shows which areas use it for backgrounds or sprites.
- **Sprites:** for each area, the player's 8 poses plus every enemy/NPC sprite that area places. They use that area's own graphics banks and palettes and play their animation frames at 60 fps. A toggle shows all 256 sprite records, and the session-25 sprite sheet sits below for comparison.
- **Areas / Screens:** the 213 area maps, the 14 title and menu pages, and the 259 screen pages, with a filter box.
- **Sound:** all 103 tracks (29 music, 74 sound effects). You can mute individual channels, and the first 40 note events of each channel are listed.
- **Text:** the 511 messages and the 4 word/name tables, with search.

**New files**
- `tools/catalog_export.py` writes the raw graphics data (`web/data/chr.bin`) and a list of all the rendered images with their notes (`web/data/catalog_manifest.json`).
- `web/catalog.html` and `web/catalog.js` are the page itself.
- `web/apu_preview.js` turns the sound data into audio. It follows the driver's envelope, vibrato (including the original's off-by-one bug), rest/hold and drum rules. It's only a preview, though: it skips pitch sweeps and fades, and plays sound effects on their own. The exact sound driver is still the Ph7 web 7/8 task.
- `tools/json_peek.py` prints the structure of a JSON file.

**Testing**
- `node web/test/catalog_check.mjs` loads the page with stand-ins for the browser and opens every tab against the real data. All 7 tabs open with no errors.
- `node web/test/apu_preview_check.mjs` renders all 103 tracks. None are broken (no invalid samples, no clipping), and the only silent one is music `$00`, which is meant to be silent. Adding `--wav music 0x13 out.wav` saves a track as a WAV file.
