**What I found** (new section "World interaction" in REVERSE.md, under Key Findings):
- **Wall and floor collision uses its own table.** The table session 23 decoded, `MetatileAttr`, only holds tile palettes. Collision comes from a 256-byte page per tileset in bank 9 (`9:B300-BDFF`). Each bit of a tile's byte means one thing:
  - b0: pit
  - b1: low obstacle (blocks walking, can be jumped)
  - b2: wall (blocks even mid-jump)
  - b3: tile that changes once the screen is visited (opened walls)
  - b4: drawn in front of sprites
  - b5: conveyor
  - b6: sink
  - b7: damage floor

  The new `tools/bg_coll_dump.py` writes all the pages to `docs/bg_coll.txt`. This also settles an old question: `9:B300-BDFF` is live data, not filler.
- **Movement blocking** (`26:9947`): each object is tested as an 8×8 box at a few points per direction (table `26:9907`). One quirk: the down-left direction never tests its top-left corner. The player also can't move past fixed screen-edge margins.
- **Player damage** (`26:9357`): damage = enemy attack − player defence, then 30 frames of flicker invulnerability and knockback.
  - If defence ≥ attack, or the result is 128 or more, nothing happens except a sound.
  - Enemy shots can cause paralysis or stone, or transform the player. Touching a poisonous enemy gives a 1-in-16 chance of poison.
  - The armor and shield can block some of these, and some objects act as platforms that carry the player over pits.
- **Per-frame status routine** (`31:EF55`): poison and damage floors cost 4 HP; certain items regenerate HP or MP while standing still; pits are detected here; invulnerability counts down. Death (HP 0) is checked at `30:CB84`.
- **Area entry and pits**: `31:E3D9` is the area-entry jump table, one of the dispatch tables earlier sessions couldn't trace. A pit slides the player down, then uses the k5 link table to load the area below. The player drops in from above, which corrects session 44: `JumpHeightTable` entries `$21-$30` are this fall-in, not a "rising exit".
- **Camera** (`31:E9AD`, `31:EA72`): dead zones and a maximum speed of 3 px per frame; with no left/right input it drifts back at 1 px per frame. It is clamped to the area edges, with 40 extra lines at the bottom because the HUD covers that part of the view.
- `$0713`, `$0714` and `$0716` are the equipped armor, shield and accessory.
