**What the routine does:**
- It turns the boss slot into the reward drop (type `$0F`) at x `$80`, y `$90`.
- It finds the area in `BossAreaList` and stores that index as the drop's phase.
- It applies that boss's 21-byte record: music, the drop's item, palette ids, CHR banks and `$62FE`. Some records also turn the remaining enemies in slots `$0E-$1E` into type `$D5`.
- It finishes with `PaletteBuild`.

**The record hooks:**
- **Ported and tested:** the area `$1A` hook (`15:B8CF`), the area `$9F` hook (`15:B8EE`) and the area `$5F` hook (`15:B947`).
- **Ported but untested:** `BossHookA6` (area `$A6`). It waits for a frame, and the test capture skips any case that does.
- **Not ported:** `BossFleeSpeech` (`15:BA98`), used by areas `$28`, `$6E` and `$F2`. It needs `ShowMessage`, which the port doesn't have yet, so it only logs "not ported". It and the `BossHookA6` check are listed as still to do in the task text.

**Quirks found:**
- The `$FF` list entry's record pointer is `$0000`, so that record would be read from zero page.
- The `$D5` loop doesn't skip the drop's own slot. A boss that died in slot `$0E` or later would turn its own drop into an enemy. Bosses die in slot `$0D`, so this doesn't happen in play.
