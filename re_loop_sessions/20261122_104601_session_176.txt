I fixed the 1-frame sound offset that showed up whenever the port started from a saved state. The low-priority boss-AI task stays open with that item crossed off.

**Cause.** `Machine.loadState` threw away the IRQ split that the previous NMI had scheduled. So the first frame after a load ran no IRQ handler. In gameplay, the HUD IRQ handler `IrqHandler1Hud` is where the sound driver ticks (`SoundUpdate` `31:F4CF`). Every loaded run therefore lost one tick for good, and every note change landed one frame after tools.emu. Runs from power-on never load a state, which is why they matched.

**Fix.** A new `loadSplits` in `web/src/game/machine.js` rebuilds that split from RAM for states taken during vblank (line > 240). Every NMI mode leaves the handler number in `$56` and the latch in `$57`. Mode 5 is the exception (handler 6, latch from `$58`). Mode 3, and modes 0/2/7 with rendering off, schedule no split.
