**(e) Menu strings in bank 16**
- The menus have their own printer at `16:8933` with 59 records at `16:8C1B`. Each record holds a screen position and a string pointer.
- Strings use four special bytes: `FF` ends the string, `FE` prints a save slot's name, `FD` prints a save slot's level, and `FC` prints an item name.
- The strings are the shop, inn, pawn shop, drop/use and save/load texts.
- **`$0421` is now confirmed as the player level (0-16).** The menu prints it as the save slot's level, which is saved from `$0421`. Session 13 had only guessed this.
- The two save slots are at `$7100` and `$7700`, each with a backup copy at `$7400` and `$7A00`.
