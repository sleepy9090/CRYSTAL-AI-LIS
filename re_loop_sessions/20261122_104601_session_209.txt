**(e) HUD rows 23-29 differing after a state load: fixed, and not caused by state loads.**
- **Cause:** all of it was one screen line, 191. REVERSE.md had already described this stripe as normal emulator behaviour (triage (g)); the port just didn't reproduce it.
- **Port change:** an IRQ handler in the port can now schedule a PPU write at a given dot within the line (`ppu.timed` in `web/src/nes/ppu.js`). Lines with scheduled writes are drawn the way tools.emu draws them, tile fetch by tile fetch.
- **Handlers using it:** the HUD handler, plus the two message-window handlers (`31:F4E5` / `31:F54B`), which switch graphics banks partway through the line below the window.
- **Timing correction:** the graphics-bank switches take effect 9 dots earlier than REVERSE.md said. tools.emu applies them from the start of the store instruction, not the actual write.
