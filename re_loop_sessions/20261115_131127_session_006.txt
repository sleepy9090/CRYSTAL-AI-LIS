**How the game picks sprite graphics** (from the disassembly):
- The sprite graphics banks are kept in two sets of shadow copies: `$07F0-$07F5`, written to the mapper by `31:F883`, and `$58-$5D`, written by `31:F64F` inside an interrupt handler.
- Player bank R2 = `$40`, or `$41` when `$0713` is nonzero (`30:C0B8`). R3 = `$41` + `$0711` clamped to 1–5, so `$42`–`$46`.
- R4/R5 are loaded per area by `31:E16D`, which reads a record through a table at `$9300`. The bank 15 code that swaps in boss music (`$12`) also writes R4/R5.
