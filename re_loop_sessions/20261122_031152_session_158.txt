**What the routine does:**
- Only one object type uses it: `$0F`, the reward item a boss leaves behind (with AI `$6E`). Its `obj_phase` is the boss's index in `BossAreaList`.
- If spawn param bit 7 is set, the object turns into type `$B3` (AI `$70` phase 1) through `FarCall_C25D`.
- Otherwise it waits until every other object in slots `$0D-$1E` is gone or is one of a few harmless kinds (AI `$78`/`$70`/`$7F`/`$30`/`$39`).
- Then it restarts the area music once, sets the drop's collision and sprite, and applies a 5-byte record for that boss from a new table, `RewardRecs` `15:B7C1` (14 records).
- Each record sets sprite palette 3, sprite CHR bank R5, the tile offset and a pending message, and rebuilds the palette.
- The three bosses that flee instead of dying (areas `$28`, `$6E`, `$F2`) are the only ones that queue a message here.
- Two quirks: phase 3 belongs to the unused `$FF` list entry and would re-apply its record every frame; and every path except the `$B3` one keeps the camera locked for `$10` frames.
