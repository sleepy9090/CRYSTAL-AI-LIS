**What was built**
- **Solution:** `ReC.slnx` with four projects: `ReC.Core`, `ReC.Run`, `ReC.Sdl` (an empty Program for now) and `ReC.Tests`. All target net10.0 with nullable on and warnings treated as errors. The Release build has 0 warnings.
- **Data and RAM names:** `tools/gen_cs_data.py` writes `Gen/R.cs` from `ram_labels.json` (368 labels). `Data.cs` loads `web/data/` and checks file sizes against the manifest.
- **Core ports:** `Game/Ram.cs`, `Game/Prg.cs`, and all of `nes/ppu.js` in `Nes/Ppu.cs`, including the mid-line timed writes.
- **Machine:** `Machine.cs` covers all of machine.js: Reset, LoadState with the first-frame IRQ split, RunFrame, and the lag-model bookkeeping.
- **Ported ahead of T3:** all of mainloop.js (`Game/MainLoop.cs`, the NMI and all IRQ handlers), plus `Game/LagModel.cs` and `Game/Palette.cs`. The first frame needs the HUD split, which made this the simplest route.
- **Runner:** `ReC.Run` takes the same options as run.mjs and writes the same CSV, PNG and dumps. It also accepts `--data DIR`.
- **Helpers:** `Util/{Png, Csv, StateFile}.cs` hold the PNG writer, CRC32, key-script parser, compare-CSV columns and state reader.
- **Stubs:** every callee not ported yet sits in its future file, without a `js:` tag, and logs `not ported: <module> <name>` once.
- **Tools:** `tools/cs_vs_web.py` runs both ports and diffs them. Options are `--canon`, `--states`, `--only KINDS`, `--frames N` and `NAME:FRAMES`. I also added `tools/state_peek.py`, which prints a state file's fields and RAM bytes.
